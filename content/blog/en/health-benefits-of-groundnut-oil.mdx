Groundnut oil has been part of Indian kitchens for generations. Here is what it offers beyond taste.

## 1. Rich in good fats

Nearly half the fat in groundnut oil is monounsaturated (MUFA), the same family of fats found in olive oil. Diets that replace saturated fat with unsaturated fat are linked to better heart health.

## 2. A natural source of vitamin E

Vitamin E is an antioxidant that helps protect cells. Cold-pressed groundnut oil keeps more of it because it is not exposed to high heat.

## 3. No cholesterol, no trans fat

Like all plant oils, groundnut oil contains no cholesterol. Pure groundnut oil has no trans fat either.

## 4. Stable at cooking temperatures

Its high smoke point means it holds up well for tadka, sautéing and frying.

## 5. Flavour that needs less

A good groundnut oil adds its own nutty aroma, so simple food tastes fuller.

> Oil is still a concentrated source of energy. Use any oil in moderation, and speak to your doctor about specific dietary needs.
