Both oils come from the same groundnut. The difference is in what happens after the seed is pressed.

## How they are made

**Cold-pressed (kachi ghani)** oil is extracted slowly at low temperature and then simply filtered. Nothing else is done to it.

**Refined** oil is pressed and then processed further to remove colour, smell and impurities.

## How they compare

|                    | Cold-pressed      | Refined     |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | ----------- |
| Taste and aroma    | Nutty, pronounced | Neutral     |
| Colour             | Deep golden       | Pale yellow |
| Nutrients retained | More              | Fewer       |
| Shelf life         | Shorter           | Longer      |
| Price              | Higher            | Lower       |

## Which one to choose

- Choose **cold-pressed** for daily home cooking, tadka and dishes where you want the flavour of the oil.
- Choose **refined** for large-volume frying, or when you want the oil to stay in the background.
- **Filtered** oil sits between the two: unrefined, with a fuller taste, at a moderate price.

Many households keep two oils: cold-pressed for everyday food and refined for frying on festive days.
