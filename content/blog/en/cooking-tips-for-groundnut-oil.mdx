A few small habits make a real difference to how your food tastes and how long your oil lasts.

1. **Heat it gently.** Warm the oil on a medium flame. If it smokes, it is too hot and the flavour suffers.
2. **Test before frying.** Drop in a small piece of batter. If it rises steadily with bubbles, the oil is ready.
3. **Do not crowd the kadhai.** Too much food at once cools the oil and makes snacks greasy.
4. **Use cold-pressed oil for tadka.** Its aroma comes through best in dals, sabzis and chutneys.
5. **Strain used oil.** Once cool, strain it through a fine cloth and store it in a closed container.
6. **Limit reuse.** Reuse frying oil once or twice at most, and never mix used oil into a fresh pack.
7. **Store it right.** Keep oil away from the stove and sunlight, with the cap closed.

## A quick guide by dish

- **Dal tadka, sabzi, thepla:** cold-pressed
- **Farsan, puri, bhajiya:** filtered or refined
- **Pickles:** cold-pressed or filtered

Good oil and a little care are most of what good food needs.
